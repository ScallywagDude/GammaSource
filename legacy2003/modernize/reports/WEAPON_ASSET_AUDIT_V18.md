## Status interpretation

- `verified_file`: exact-case file lookup only; the separate content/format state governs what was actually decoded.
- `unsupported_version` with MDL 37: skeletons, animations and model material assignments were not decoded or certified.
- `verified_rectangle`: configured crop fits the parsed texture dimensions; it is not proof of matching artwork.
- `pixels_verified_nonblank`: supported texture pixels were decoded and the selected crop contains visible pixels. This remains separate from material/shader operation and artwork identity.
- Font-glyph states identify source-unsupported roles or the separately supplied generated monochrome support atlas; generated glyph artwork is replacement UI support, not restored original artwork.
- No CS/TF2 asset directories were supplied. Their exact registered script names and implementation/build states are listed, while models/materials/HUD assets remain unverified.
- Individual VVD/PHY/QC or alternate VTX variants are not automatically mandatory for this early model format; companion/layout compatibility still requires a supported model and renderer tests.
